Privacy Policy

Rath Wars™ (रथ वॉर्स) · Last updated: November 26, 2026

Rath Wars™ is an independent game developed and published by Anmol Biswas (shadowangel). We respect your privacy and collect only the minimal information required to run optional online features and verify Full Edition purchases.

1. No Ads & No Tracking

  • Rath Wars™ contains no third-party advertisements and no ad-tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • Offline modes (Campaign, Play vs AI, Two Players, Koshagar, and Records) work entirely on your device without sending personal data to any server.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Game settings (audio volume, narration language, controls), campaign progress, achievements, chariot customization, and Full Edition unlock status are saved locally in your device's app storage.

3. Optional Online Duel & Friends List

If you choose to use Online Duel, the game connects to our relay server (rathwars-relay.rathwars.workers.dev) and stores only:

  • Your chosen in-game display name and random numeric tag (for example, Arjuna#1234).
  • A randomly generated device account ID and secret token stored on your device.
  • Your friends list, friend requests, blocked list, and temporary match room state.
  • A one-way salted cryptographic hash of your IP address, used solely to prevent spam sign-ups and abuse (the raw IP address is not stored).

How to delete your online profile: Open Online Duel → Delete my online profile inside the game at any time. This immediately clears your friends, requests, blocks, and challenges and hides your profile from search. You may also email shadowangelx2x5@gmail.com to request manual deletion.

4. Full Edition Purchases & Payments

Optional one-time Full Edition (₹49 INR) purchases are processed securely by Razorpay (UPI, cards, and supported payment methods). We never receive, process, or store your UPI PIN, bank account credentials, or full card details. To activate your unlock and support Restore Purchase across devices, our server stores only the Razorpay payment reference ID, unlock key, and a salted hash of the contact email/phone provided at checkout.

5. Device Permissions

On Android, Rath Wars™ requests only standard network permissions (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) for Online Duel, purchase verification, and updates. We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs, or GPS location.

6. Children's Privacy

Rath Wars™ is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 18 where required by applicable law without parental consent). If you believe a child has submitted personal data through support or checkout, contact us and we will promptly remove it.

7. Changes & Contact
