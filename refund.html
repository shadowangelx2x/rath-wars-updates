1. One-Time Digital Purchase (No Subscriptions)

The Rath Wars™ Full Edition (₹49 INR) is a one-time digital unlock, not a recurring subscription. You will never be billed automatically again, so no subscription cancellation is required.

2. Non-Refundable Digital Goods

Because players can freely try Rath Wars™ before buying — including unlimited Play vs AI, Two Players, Campaign Days 1–3, and joining Online Duels — all Full Edition digital unlock purchases are final and non-refundable once delivered and activated.

3. Eligible Refund Exceptions

We gladly resolve payment errors and issue a full refund in the following genuine cases:

  • Duplicate Charge: You were accidentally charged more than once for the same Full Edition unlock.
  • Failed Activation: Money was deducted from your UPI/bank/card, the game did not unlock, and our support team is unable to activate your Unlock Key after verifying the transaction.

4. How to Request Help or an Eligible Refund

Please email shadowangelx2x5@gmail.com within 7 days of the transaction with:

  • Your Razorpay Payment ID or UPI UTR / reference number
  • The email address or phone number used at checkout
  • A brief note on whether you want us to activate your Unlock Key or refund a duplicate/failed charge

5. Refund Processing Time

Once verified and approved, refunds are initiated via Razorpay back to your original payment method (UPI / bank account / card) and typically settle within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or UPI provider.
